## Preserved runtime attempt

Processes passed with the exact expected output, root exit 0, two fixture-origin SIGKILL adoptions, no owner signals and final ECHILD. Verify then exited 0 after its closure, catalog, diagnostic, malformed-request, quota and failed-publication assertions. Its 329-byte closure matched the frozen digest `08b7ec6ca8d209820e44ddbda3151b5f8b866334fd45cc7f06124794279f3dc9`.

The controller incorrectly expected Verify's PASS line on stdout. Native `os.Print` delegates to POSIX Print, which writes fd2; the exact 85-byte stderr line was correct. The controller stopped before launching the C consumer and returned failure after 3.132 seconds to final-write start, with complete cleanup. Source/runtime/artifact fences were independently rechecked, and only the two completed phases were admitted. The complete failed attempt was preserved without changing its result. Output-oracle reviews must trace the actual API to its stream rather than infer the stream from a PASS message.
