https://github.com/blender/blender/blob/v4.3.2/source/blender/bmesh/operators/bmo_primitive.cc

stock-suzanne.json preserves the exact source vertex and face arrays emitted by
Blender 4.3.2. convert.py records source and derived geometry SHA-256 digests,
applies two Catmull-Clark subdivision levels, caps the four remaining boundary
loops, triangulates, and samples the union of three closed components.
The reference geometry and derived field atlases are distributed under the same
GPL-2.0-or-later terms. This is not a claim that stock Suzanne is watertight.
