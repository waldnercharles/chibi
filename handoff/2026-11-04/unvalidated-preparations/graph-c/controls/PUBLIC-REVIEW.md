## Source decisions and corrected findings

- Removed the first separate core-owner loop after deriving the production
  caller, so there is only one owner lifecycle implementation in these controls.
- Reserved fixture fd3/fd4 before opening the executable, avoiding descriptor
  overwrite in the child. Child-only pipe ends close in the parent; terminal
  evidence is captured before final descriptor close.
- Suppression/wrong-subtree cases wait for the required genuine registered
  ancestry before releasing the synthetic cancellation. Wrong-argv/adoption
  cases deliberately do not borrow that barrier. No such barrier touches18/19.
- The suite does not put a five-second outer reserve inside each eight-second
  case. One unchanged outer spans420s. Its sole worker enforces per-case clocks
  and disables only its own subreaper role for the final predeclared case34.
- Final case34 keeps durable prior ECHILD results and known fixture identities.
  The top-level controller preserves the outer's nonzero return; an external
  coordinator must record the actual top-level exit. A function result is not
  mislabeled as an observed process exit.
- Missing/changed admissions, unexpected children, incomplete cleanup, late
  completion and unrecognized terminal outcomes are failures, never retries.
- Case13 includes supported POLLIN/HUP retirement positives and independent
  invalid/error/shape negatives against the fresh narrow ready() successor.
  The earlier candidate remains unchanged. This is a read-boundary regression,
  not evidence that this kernel produced an invalid pidfd event.

## What I would delete

Nothing from production or earlier proofs. Keep the two mutants separate because
their exact changed bytes are the counterfactual evidence. No old owner loop or
unused duplicate oracle remains in the control binding.

## What I would measure

Actual proc topology/count, max full-scan latency and scheduling gap, real20ms
registered versus missed outcome, native compilation/runtime closure size,
admission hashing overhead, terminal write/fsync/close latency, actual normal/
cleanup/external headroom and final unchanged-outer completion. No speed or
registration reliability claim is supported by this source-only preparation.

## What I would leave alone

Frozen CTests/Capture/POSIX behavior; prior proof packets and runtime refusals;
original accepted core; unchanged outer bytes; production timing; native build
sources; repository/branch state. The fresh ready() delta belongs to its own
independent review and is pinned exactly here, not silently substituted.
