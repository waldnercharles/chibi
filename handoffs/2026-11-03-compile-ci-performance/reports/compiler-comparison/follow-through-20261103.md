# Prior art applied to the measured Chibi work

The comparisons use the pinned revisions in the original comparison report. External compilers were not benchmarked; their toolchains, optimization defaults and coverage differ. The conclusions below concern implementation patterns and measured Chibi work.

Chibi measurements now narrow the LLVM question. The diagnostic closure contains98 files/3.42MB; building its probe took51.70s, while all23 scenarios ran in0.294s. Existing emit-only mode produced81.61MB/55 pieces in11.10s. That path also joins output and omits tool validation, so it is not an exact prefix duration of the regular build.

For one retained4.68MB module, opt took3.54s and llc4.39s. Its reported IR parsing CPU was0.0716/0.0319s; SROA and register allocation/coalescing were larger measured work. These module-specific observations do not establish full-pipeline percentages or process-startup overhead. They support inspecting generated slots/live ranges before changing the LLVM interface.

The retained bundle has341,749 allocas. Its largest `Lower` function has16,901. Byte-identical repeated helpers add3.72MB, but the largest are live internal per-module helpers; conservatively unused helpers are only0.295% of bundle bytes. No material speed prediction justifies a linkage rewrite from those counts.

Already integrated compiler changes remain the original small changes: read-only type-table access, primitive-field ownership avoidance, and demand-based pointer construction. Their separate measured source epochs and limits remain in `comparison.md`. Core candidate reuse and mandatory native command contracts now have independent review and real correctness evidence. Controlled composed-head rebuild/edit/test measurements and complete CI latency/runner work remain outstanding.

The engineering-review briefs, operation counts, regressions, ordered review passes and exact receipts are under `native-command-proof-6699/`. The next backend experiment is a bounded emission change using existing semantic IDs and validation facts, with addressed/managed/task/floating values and GC roots excluded from its initial eligibility.
